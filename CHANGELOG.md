## [0.1.0] - 2026-06-12

Initial release. QR / barcode scanner for Mob apps.

- `MobScanner.start/2` / `stop/1`. Requires the `mob_camera` plugin for the capture pipeline.
- Extracted from mob core in the 0.7.0 plugin-extraction wave.
- Requires `mob ~> 0.7`.
